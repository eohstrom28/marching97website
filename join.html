Want to Join the Marching 97?

We are so glad you're interested in our psyche-filled band! The Marching 97 is open to any...

  • Newly Admitted Student
  • Transfer Student
  • Current Lehigh Student
  • LVAIC Student

No prior experience needed!

While being in the 97 can take up a decent amount of time, the band is very close-knit and cherishes many unique traditions, both old and new. Members of the Marching 97 have plenty of time to lead active social lives, participate in other extracurricular activities, and keep their grades up!
