The Manager can be contacted at marching97manager@gmail.com.

The Drum Major

The Marching 97 Drum Major is responsible for running field practice and leading the band in performances. The Drum Major writes the first field show each season and organizes drill writing for the rest of the performances. Look for the Drum Major in their tall white hat as they lead the band in the traditional Marching LEHIGH! The Drum Major can be contacted at DrumMajor@marching97.org.

The Freshmen Managers

The Freshmen Managers, or "Refrosh", of the Marching 97 are responsible for the recruitment and retention of the 97's members. Besides organizing ways to keep up morale and excitement, the Refrosh are in charge of planning the Marching 97's involvement in admitted students events, providing information to prospective band members, and helping the incoming class and new members through their first season with the 97. (You can join the Marching 97 in any year of your college career!)
